## Product Purpose
A Russian-language landing page for ООО «КАСТОМС ЛИДЕР», turning equipment transportation inquiries into requests for cost and timing.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve customer-facing text, facts, meanings and section order from landing-structure.md. Three inline forms, two modal forms, service preselection, four journey tabs, eight FAQ items. No uploads. Bitrix24 is not connected. Local delivery only. Legal documents and real founder photo remain client-dependent, not invented approvals.

## Brand Commitments
The user specified a cinematic scroll story: empty low-loader platform, changing loads, journey and winter-road imagery, globe, return to empty platform. The visual narrative must not introduce Norda's conflicting facts or new slogans. Build approach delegated to the implementer; implement directly in code to author the actual scroll behavior.

## Product Principles
Content remains accessible independently of the 3D scene. Original facts outrank illustrative storytelling. Forms must not claim a successful CRM submission without a working connection. Motion supports the route and respects reduced-motion preferences.
